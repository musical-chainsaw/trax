TRAX

How To

TRAX lets you visualize your past activities on a map. You can customize colors and decide which sports to show. This tutorial focuses on data import from STRAVA.
However, GPX data can be retrieved from almost any tracking device or app.

TAKES ABOUT 10 MINUTES TO SET UP. WAIT TIME FROM SOME MINUTES UP TO SEVERAL HOURS.

How To Start

WARNING: TRAX can be very demanding on your hardware.

  • On your PC, go to strava.com and log in.
  • Navigate to Settings (below the profile picture) > My Account, scroll down and click Download or Delete Your Account.
    Alternatively, go to strava.com/delete_your_account.
  • After Requesting Your Archive, unzip the file.
  • Go to musical-chainsaw.github.io/trax and either click on the field AUTO UPLOAD or drag-and-drop your selected files to that field.
  • It will take some moments to analyze.
  • Some activities may not be recognized. They will appear on the map once you allocate them to a sport type (upper-left tool bar).
  • In the archive, if you have noticed files other that .gpx, go to the archive folder and open the file activities.csv.
    There, search for the non-gpx filename.
    With the information related to that workout, you can go to strava.com/athlete/training and look for that entry by searching.
    Click on the title > ... > Export GPX.
  • If there is still some routes missing, double-check by manuallly re-downloading that specific route directly, as described in the step before.

Additional Features

  • IMPORT ROUTES

    Click on AUTO UPLOAD and select all your files. Don't use the same file twice as this will result in false heat data.

  • ELEVATION INSIGHT

    Click on ELVATION at the bottom to see which parts of the routes had high elevation.

  • SAVE AS IMAGE

    To capture a high-resolution PNG, click on CAPTURE at the bottom. Before, zoom in on the map to your desired crop. You can adjust the scale and type of glow.

  • FOCUS

    Click on FOCUS to enter fullscreen and hide the menubar. Navigate back in your browser's controls to exit.

  • EXPORT

    Export your currently visible routes as a .trax file. It can only be imported in TRAX. Make sure to toggle on all sport types you want to include. Only open .trax files with our website as they could crash your device due to their size.

  • COMPARE

    Lets you import your or a friend's routes. To compare, you have to first export and import your own routes as a .trax file, then also import the one you want to compare with.

You can also save your heatmap as an image.

  • Click on Save in the bottom-left corner. You may have to confirm the download or save it manually.

More

All GPX data are being stored locally.
After closing and reopening the site, your data will not be lost.
Data will be deleted after uninstalling your browser, deleting your browsing history, deleting site data, or being in private mode.
No data is leaving your device.
